# MVP: Probably Fine: Saturday

## Goal

Ship one replayable Saturday run across five authored scenes in approximately 15-20 minutes. The first five minutes should already contain a meaningful label/information puzzle and a funny consequence. The complete route is Grocery Store -> Food Truck -> Uncle Rick's BBQ -> The Date -> The Biscuit.

## Starting state

The first tuning pass can use a $40 budget, midrange hunger, low condition load, and one compact condition profile containing a true food allergy, an intolerance, and an environmental trigger. These numbers and exact conditions are tuning values, not permanent canon. Start each scene with a clear immediate goal and at least two viable paths.

## Required systems

- Resource display and state transitions for money, hunger, satisfaction/boredom, time, condition load, and inventory.
- Hidden actual food/environment facts with separate player knowledge.
- Inspect/ask/test/discover actions that cost time or money and change knowledge.
- Ingredient and cross-contact evaluation; dose-based intolerance effects; environmental hazards.
- Relevant aids such as lactase/alpha-galactosidase style enzymes for intolerances, with limited uses. No allergy protection from these aids.
- A clearly labeled "Risk It for the Biscuit" gamble near the climax, with a meaningful safe alternative.
- End-of-run recap with consequences and discoveries, plus restart.

## Scene acceptance

Each of the five scenes must contain a physical-looking interactive space, at least three inspectable/clickable elements, at least one short NPC interaction, one actionable uncertainty, at least two materially different outcomes, and a joke or visual beat tied to the scene. A scene can be brief; it should not be a dressed-up modal questionnaire.

## Overall acceptance

- A first-time player can finish a run without reading a manual.
- The Grocery Store is fun as a stand-alone vertical slice before the next scene is built.
- Consequences reflect actual condition rules and the information available at decision time.
- There is a viable, satisfying low-risk route and a tempting, legible risky route.
- The game works with mouse and touch at common laptop and phone sizes.
- No real medical treatment is presented as making allergen exposure safe.
- Resume, restart, mute, and reduced-motion settings work by the complete MVP gate.

## Out of scope

Jobs, apartments, daily schedules, town map, infinite procedural events, character creation depth, multiplayer, cloud saves, and in-game medical advice.
