# Saturday scene map

These are authored starting points. Keep the interactive objects and comic pressure; tune exact items, condition profile, and prices after playing the Grocery slice.

## 1. Grocery Store

**Goal:** buy something edible before the day gets busy. **Pressure:** finite cash, rising hunger, a closing checkout line.

Interactive props: a bright front-of-pack promise, tiny ingredient label, bakery case, sample tray, shelf of safe-but-dull staples, and checkout. The player can inspect labels, ask a clerk about bakery handling, compare a cheaper uncertain snack with a pricier reliable one, use a plausible test where appropriate, or leave with nothing. A "may contain" clue and bakery cross-contact matter independently of the ingredient list.

Comic beat: the package has six reassuring claims in huge type and one useful fact in the smallest type physically possible. Keep the useful text readable in the UI.

Outcomes: satisfying safe purchase; cheap risk with a consequence; cautious purchase that costs time/money; skipped meal that raises hunger for Food Truck. First playable target: a 3-5 minute loop with a clear restart.

## 2. Food Truck

**Goal:** get lunch before the queue steals the afternoon. **Pressure:** speed, social awkwardness, hunger.

Interactive props: menu board, condiment bottles, shared fryer, cash window, cook, queue. Ask about ingredients and shared equipment, choose a substitute, use an intolerance aid for a relevant side, or decide the fryer warning is worth the risk. The cook's answer can be helpful but incomplete while the visible fryer provides stronger evidence.

Comic beat: the menu loudly lists "fresh" while the fryer quietly provides the most important information in the scene.

Outcomes: safe but slower meal; tempting contaminated meal; no meal and extra cash; lunch choice that influences energy and the BBQ arrival time.

## 3. Uncle Rick's BBQ

**Goal:** eat and survive a family visit without turning it into a courtroom. **Pressure:** Uncle Rick's confidence, limited polite exits, outdoor triggers.

Interactive props: grill, mystery marinade, cooler, side dishes, ingredient packaging, cat near the patio, pollen-heavy yard, Uncle Rick. Ask Rick, inspect packages, bring/use a packed alternative, choose seating away from environmental triggers, or decline a dish. Rick should be loveable and sincerely inaccurate. His dialogue is a clue source with unreliable confidence, never the ground truth.

Comic beat: Rick has three different versions of the recipe, all told with identical certainty. One version includes a small ingredient he calls "basically air."

Outcomes: safe social win, awkward but healthy exit, avoidable exposure, environmental discomfort, relationship reaction shaped by how the player handled the conversation.

## 4. The Date

**Goal:** have a good time while being honest enough to make a sound choice. **Pressure:** satisfaction/boredom, time, social confidence, remaining money.

Interactive props: venue menu, drink/snack, server, date, nearby fallback option. Ask a direct question, propose a different place, eat a known option, or take a risky shortcut to keep the moment flowing. The date has agency; they can respond well to a clear explanation and poorly to evasive chaos, without making illness a moral failing.

Comic beat: a meticulously arranged romantic plate contains an ingredient the player now recognizes instantly from earlier label sleuthing.

Outcomes: surprisingly good honest date, rushed workaround, expensive safe pivot, or a memorable disaster created by a known gamble.

## 5. The Biscuit

**Goal:** decide what kind of Saturday this has been. **Pressure:** the most attractive food of the run, limited cash/time, accumulated condition load.

Interactive props: display biscuit, seller, ingredient note, preparation area, alternative snack, "Risk It for the Biscuit" action. There must be a way to investigate and a meaningful safe choice. The risky action is a deliberate commitment with the unresolved facts shown first, not an accidental click trap.

Comic beat: the biscuit receives absurdly heroic framing while the label remains mundane and suspicious.

Outcomes: multiple short endings keyed to earlier choices, including a competent safe win, a messy-but-okay compromise, and a consequence-rich biscuit gamble. The recap names why events happened and what the player learned; restart is one action.
